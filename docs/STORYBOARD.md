# Storyboard

The story is implemented in `src/data/scenes.ts`. Scenes contain presenter-controlled beats; Space/Arrow Right advances one beat, not an entire scene.

1. Meet Hantira: dark entrance, business assets, his objection to insurance.
2. One-Time QR Join: `شارك حنتيرة الرحلة`, room code, QR, live participant count.
3. Hantira's Opinion: audience votes whether Hantira is right.
4. The Nice Morning: Monday drive, crash, invoice count-up concept.
5. Who Pays?: conceptual coverage-dependent answer.
6. What Is Risk?: Risk, cost, simplified Probability x Impact framing.
7. Insurance in 30 Seconds: risk transfer through Premium and policy wording.
8. Hantira Goes Shopping: fictional insurer offers overwhelm him.
9. Choose the Offer: three fictional prices; Hantira points at the cheapest.
10. Broker Challenge: price is not enough; right fit is best.
11. Complexity Explosion: Premium, Deductible, Limits, Exclusions, Claims.
12. Meet Faheem: broker enters as independent clarity, not an insurer representative.
13. What Does a Broker Do?: needs, risks, design, market, comparison, negotiation, management, claims, renewal.
14. Hantira Logistics: 30 trucks, warehouse, employees, international shipments.
15. Build Protection: multi-select coverage thinking; shield visual.
16. Six Months Later: stylized incident and alarm.
17. Claim Challenge: first claim action, with policy-dependent caveat.
18. Claims Journey: notification, documentation, survey, review, settlement.
19. Claims Chaos: coordination role without replacing insurer/surveyor/client responsibilities.
20. Insurance Value: value is felt after loss.
21. Broker Value: advice, comparison, negotiation, coordination, support.
22. With Broker / Without Broker: interactive simplification slider.
23. Final Understanding: company, broker, or both.
24. What Our Company Does: configurable placeholder company flow.
25. Final Hantira: business protected, Hantira understands insurance as protection.
26. Ending and Post-Credit Joke: prepare for risk, company tagline, Premium installment joke.

The product requirement says “18 core story scenes”; the implementation preserves all requested beats and interactions, represented as 26 scene entries so interactions and ending moments remain controllable.
